# PDR-002 — Delivery days replace Sales Days, and catalog backlog

**Status:** Proposal (revised 2026-11-01)
**Language:** English
**Scope:** Next feature after Version 1
**Document role:** Product design requirement. Business source of truth for this feature. Not a description of the running API.
**Supersedes, for this feature only:** every Version 1 rule built on the Sales Day: opening and closing a day, editing only while the day is `OPEN`, reopening only today, the seller hub of recent Sales Days, invoices listed per Sales Day, and the Load Sheet of one closed Sales Day. The earlier draft of this PDR (current week of Sales Days, week edit window) is also superseded. Price snapshots, customer identity, and thermal printing stay as in [PDR.md](./PDR.md).

## 1. Purpose

The seller does not work in capture days. The seller sells whenever the customer orders, and everything after the sale is organized by **when the goods are delivered**: what to load, which invoices to print, and which sales to correct when the store returns product or asks for one more item.

The Sales Day (open, close, reopen) is a gate the business no longer wants. This feature removes it from the seller's work and makes the **delivery day** the only organizing unit.

It also takes the catalog work that Version 1 specified and did not build: the Excel import, the pull from Alegra, and Alegra sync on product create and delete.

## 2. What changes for the seller

1. There is nothing to open or close. A sale can be started at any time.
2. After the customer is chosen, and before any product, the seller says **when the order will be delivered**. The date is stored on the sale.
3. For a delivery day the seller can see:
   - the **Load Sheet** (products to load);
   - the **invoices** to print;
   - the **sales** of that day, to correct or remove them.
4. A correction (a return, an extra product, a quantity change, a different delivery day, or removing the sale) is made by opening the sale from its delivery day.

## 3. Terminology

| Term | Meaning in this feature |
|---|---|
| Delivery day | The business date stored on the sale. It selects the Load Sheet, the invoices to print, and the sales to correct. |
| Capture date | The business date the sale was created. Informational only. It no longer gates anything. |
| Sales Day | Removed. The concept, its routes, and its table are deleted. |
| Return | A correction of an existing sale: edit lines, or void the whole sale. No separate document. |

## 4. Delivery date

- Mandatory. A sale without it is not created.
- It is chosen from today through the Sunday that closes the current week (Monday–Sunday, business timezone). On a Sunday only Sunday is offered.
- It can be changed at any time, to a date in that same range. The sale then leaves the previous day's Load Sheet and invoice list and appears on the new day.
- A **draft** simply takes the new date.
- A **confirmed** sale is not moved. Its invoice is voided on the original day (number kept there), and a new confirmed sale is created with the same customer, seller, and lines (same unit prices and costs), the new delivery date, a new id, and a new invoice number from the new day's sequence. The seller is taken to the new sale.

## 5. Edit window

There is no edit window. A sale can be edited, confirmed, re-dated, or voided at any time, including sales of earlier weeks. Nothing opens, closes, or locks a delivery day.

Correcting a confirmed sale keeps its invoice number.

## 6. Load Sheet

The Load Sheet for a delivery day includes every confirmed sale with that delivery date. Drafts and voided sales contribute nothing. Aggregation stays by `productId`. When one product was sold at more than one unit price, the row shows the current catalog prices and lists each price group. It is computed on demand and not stored.

## 7. Invoices

The invoices to print for a delivery day are the confirmed sales with that delivery date. A voided sale stays listed and is not printable. Reprinting reads the corrected lines and the same invoice number.

Numbering is one sequence per **delivery date** (001, 002…), allocated when the sale is confirmed. Two sales of the same delivery day never share a number.

The printed invoice keeps two dates: the **invoice date** is the confirmation date, and the field labeled "Fecha de vencimiento" becomes **"Fecha de entrega"** and shows the delivery date.

## 8. Returns and additions

On the delivery day the seller opens the sale and either:

- changes quantities, adds a line, or removes a line; or
- voids the sale, which removes it from the Load Sheet and from printing.

A confirmed sale must still contain at least one line. Removing the last line is a void. The transaction-time item name stays editable only while the sale is `DRAFT`.

## 9. Catalog backlog moved here

These items do not change the sale or delivery rules.

| Item | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Excel migration | One-shot import of the current product file. Invalid and duplicate rows are reported. Values are not silently dropped or rewritten. |
| Alegra inbound | Pull items and prices from Alegra into the local catalog. |
| Create and delete sync | Creating or deleting a product updates Alegra. Update sync already exists and stays. |

## 10. Out of scope

- A return invoice, a credit note, or a negative quantity.
- Inventory.
- Offline capture.
- Printing the Load Sheet.
- A new invoice number when a confirmed sale's lines are corrected.
- Closing or locking a delivery day.
- Choosing a delivery date outside the current week.

## 11. Acceptance criteria

- A sale can be created at any time without opening anything.
- Every new sale has a persisted delivery date, captured before the first line.
- For a delivery day the seller gets the Load Sheet, the invoices, and the sales list, each by delivery date.
- A sale captured on Monday for Wednesday appears only on Wednesday's views.
- Changing the delivery date of a draft moves it between days.
- Changing the delivery date of a confirmed sale leaves a voided invoice on the old day and a new confirmed invoice, with the same lines, on the new day.
- A voided sale disappears from the Load Sheet and is not printable. A quantity change changes the aggregated quantity.
- The invoice number of a corrected confirmed sale does not change. Numbers restart per delivery date and are unique within it.
- A sale from an earlier week can still be edited or voided.
- The printed invoice shows the confirmation date and, as "Fecha de entrega", the delivery date.
- Excel import reports invalid and duplicate rows. Alegra inbound pull and create/delete sync have an application path.

## 12. Decisions (2026-11-01)

| Id | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| OQ-1 | Invoice numbering | One sequence per **delivery date**, allocated at confirm. |
| OQ-2 | Until when can a sale change | **Always**. No edit window. |
| OQ-3 | Allowed delivery dates | **Today through Sunday** of the current week. |
| OQ-4 | Delivery-day lock | **None**. |
| OQ-5 | Seller of a sale | `sellerId` on the sale, from the session. |
| OQ-6 | Where drafts are seen | On the delivery day's sales list, tagged as draft. Never on the Load Sheet or printing. |
| OQ-7 | Dates on the invoice | Invoice date = confirmation date. "Fecha de vencimiento" is relabeled **"Fecha de entrega"** and shows the delivery date. |
| OQ-8 | Existing data | Current sales, Sales Days, and invoice sequences are test data and are **deleted with a one-off command run directly on the database**, not with a migration. Customers, the product catalog, users, and company profile are kept. The `sales_days` table is dropped by the schema change. |
| OQ-9 | Re-dating a confirmed sale | Void it on the original day and create a new confirmed sale with the same data, a new id, the new delivery date, and a new number from that day's sequence. |
| OQ-10 | Customers | Test data, but **kept**. |
