# PDR-002 — Current week, delivery day, and catalog backlog

**Status:** Proposal
**Language:** English
**Scope:** Next feature after Version 1
**Document role:** Product design requirement. Business source of truth for this feature. Not a description of the running API.
**Supersedes, for this feature only:** Version 1 rules that a sale is editable only while its Sales Day is `OPEN`, that only today's Sales Day may be reopened, that the seller hub is the rolling last 7 Sales Days, and that the Load Sheet is the aggregation of one closed Sales Day. Version 1 invoice numbering, price snapshots, customer identity, and thermal printing stay as in [PDR.md](./PDR.md).

## 1. Purpose

Sellers already capture the visit in the application. Two operational facts are still outside that capture.

They correct an order after the visit, including on a later day of the same week, and they correct it again on the delivery day when the store returns product. The Load Sheet they prepare is the set of goods to deliver on a given day, not the set of invoices written on the capture day.

This feature also takes the catalog work that Version 1 specified and did not build: the Excel import, the pull from Alegra, and Alegra sync on product create and delete.

## 2. What changes for the seller

1. The hub is the **current week**, Monday through Sunday in the business timezone. It is not a rolling window of 7 days. A day from the previous week is not on this hub.
2. A sale whose Sales Day falls in the current week can be changed or voided. The week's earlier days stay editable. A Sales Day before the current week cannot be changed.
3. After the customer is chosen, and before any product is added, the seller must say **when the order will be delivered**. The question is asked for every new sale. The date is stored on the sale.
4. The Load Sheet is the confirmed sales whose delivery date is the chosen day. Capture day and open/closed status do not select the rows.
5. On the delivery day, a return is handled by opening that sale and changing it, or by removing the sale completely. There is no separate return document.

## 3. Terminology

| Term | Meaning in this feature |
|---|---|
| Current week | Monday 00:00 through Sunday of the week that contains today's business date. |
| Sales Day | Unchanged. The capture-day container. Invoice numbers stay one sequence per Sales Day date. |
| Delivery date | The business date stored on the sale. It selects the Load Sheet. |
| Return | A correction of an existing sale on its delivery day: edit lines, or void the whole sale. |

## 4. Edit window

A sale may be edited or voided when its Sales Day date is inside the current week and the day is `OPEN`. A closed day rejects those commands until the seller reopens it. A sale whose Sales Day date is before the current Monday cannot be edited, voided, or reopened.

Closing a day records that the seller finished that capture day and blocks new or changed sales. Reopening today unlocks them again.

Correcting a confirmed sale keeps its invoice number. Confirming a draft still allocates the number for the capture date, not for the delivery date.

## 5. Delivery date

The seller sets the delivery date at the start of every sale, after the customer is selected and before the first line.

Rules:

- The date is mandatory. A sale without it is not created.
- It is a business calendar date. It may be the capture day or a later day.
- It is stored on the sale and is not derived from the Sales Day.
- While the sale is inside the edit window, the seller may change the delivery date. The sale then leaves the previous day's Load Sheet and enters the new day's Load Sheet.
- A past week's sale keeps the stored date and cannot be moved.

## 6. Load Sheet

The Load Sheet for a delivery date includes every confirmed sale with that delivery date. Drafts and voided sales contribute nothing. Aggregation stays by `productId`, with the same columns as Version 1. When one product was sold at more than one unit price, the row shows the current catalog prices and lists each price group.

The sheet is available for a delivery date whether or not a Sales Day is closed. It is still not stored.

## 7. Returns

On the delivery day the seller opens the sale and either:

- changes quantities, adds a line, or removes a line; or
- voids the sale, which removes it completely from the Load Sheet and from invoice printing.

A confirmed sale must still contain at least one line. Removing the last line is a void, not an empty confirmed sale. The invoice number is not reused and is not replaced. Generating the invoice again reads the corrected lines.

The transaction-time item name stays editable only while the sale is `DRAFT`. A return does not add a name-editing step.

## 8. Catalog backlog moved here

These items leave the Version 1 pending list and belong to this feature. They do not change the sale, week, or Load Sheet rules.

| Item | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Excel migration | One-shot import of the current product file. Invalid and duplicate rows are reported. Values are not silently dropped or rewritten. |
| Alegra inbound | Pull items and prices from Alegra into the local catalog. |
| Create and delete sync | Creating or deleting a product updates Alegra. Update sync already exists and stays as it is. |

## 9. Out of scope

- A return invoice, a credit note, or a negative quantity.
- Inventory.
- Editing a sale from a previous week.
- Offline capture.
- Printing the Load Sheet.
- A new invoice number when a confirmed sale is corrected.

## 10. Acceptance criteria

- The hub lists Sales Days of the current Monday–Sunday week only.
- A confirmed sale from an earlier day of this week can change quantities, lines, delivery date, or be voided.
- The same actions on a sale from last week are rejected.
- Every new sale has a persisted delivery date, captured before the first line.
- The Load Sheet for a date lists confirmed sales with that delivery date, including sales captured on another day.
- A voided sale disappears from that Load Sheet. A quantity change changes the aggregated quantity.
- The invoice number of a corrected confirmed sale does not change.
- Excel import reports invalid and duplicate rows.
- Alegra inbound pull and create/delete sync have an application path. Update sync remains.
