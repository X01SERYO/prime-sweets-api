# PDR-004 — Daily retail sale

**Status:** Proposal
**Language:** English
**Scope:** New retail (al detal) sales channel. Independent of invoices and delivery days.
**Document role:** Product design requirement. Business source of truth for this feature. Not a description of the running API.

## 1. Purpose

Today every sale is a wholesale sale: it has a customer, a delivery date, and an invoice. The business also sells at retail over the counter, without a customer. The seller needs to record what was sold at retail each day and see the day's total sold and total profit.

A retail sale is not an invoice. It lives on its own screen.

## 2. What the seller can do

1. Open **Detal** from the main navigation.
2. See the retail sale of today. There is one retail sale per business day for the whole business. A new day starts a new, empty retail sale.
3. Search a product and pick it from a list shown under the search field. No modal opens. Picking it does **not** add it yet.
4. Type the quantity next to the picked product and press **+ Agregar**. Only then the product is added to today's list.
5. Change the quantity directly on the product row, or delete the row.
6. See the total sold and the total profit above and below the list.
7. Open the retail days: one view of days **Sin revisar** (default) and one of days **Revisados**, 10 per page. Today appears in Sin revisar once it has sales.
8. In **Sin revisar**, select one or more days, see the accumulated total sold and total profit of the selection, and with one click (**Cerrar caja**) mark all of them as **Revisado**. This action exists only in that view, never on today's sale list.

## 2.1 Review status

| Status | Code | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Sin revisar | `UNREVIEWED` | Default on creation. |
| Revisado | `REVIEWED` | Set only by the manual **Cerrar caja** action. |

- The only transition is `UNREVIEWED → REVIEWED`. `REVIEWED → UNREVIEWED` is rejected.
- An unreviewed retail sale never expires. It can be reviewed any later day, as long as its status is `UNREVIEWED`.
- Today can be reviewed. Once reviewed it is closed: nothing more can be added that day, and its product detail is discarded at that moment.
- Reviewing records who reviewed it and when. It does not change totals or the business date.
- Several days are reviewed together in one action, all or none: if any selected day is already reviewed, none changes.

## 3. Rules

- **One retail sale per business day** (`America/Bogota`) for the whole business. The date is set on creation and cannot be changed.
- **Only today is editable**, and only while it is `UNREVIEWED`. The API enforces this; the client only hides the controls.
- **Adding:** a product is added with a quantity of at least `1`, typed before confirming with **+ Agregar**. Quantities are whole numbers.
- **Adding a product that is already on the list** adds the new quantity to the existing row. A product never appears twice.
- **Row quantity** can be edited to any whole number of at least `1`. To take a product out, the seller uses the delete button.
- **Price is frozen when the product is added** (retail price and retail cost at that moment). A later catalog change does not alter the row.
- Line total = retail unit price × quantity. Line profit = (retail unit price − retail unit cost) × quantity.
- Day total sold = sum of line totals. Day total profit = sum of line profits.
- A product without retail cost can be added. Its profit counts as `0` and its row shows a warning tooltip ("Producto sin costo detal: la ganancia no se calcula").
- A product without a retail price cannot be added. A retired product is not offered.
- A retail sale has no customer, no delivery date, no invoice number, and no confirmation or void step.
- **Only today keeps the detail.** Today's product list is saved on the server so it survives closing the session or changing device. When the day changes, or when today is reviewed, the detail is discarded. A past day keeps only: date, status, total sold, total profit, and, once reviewed, who reviewed it and when.
- **A day with total sold `0` is never stored.** The day is created with its first product and deleted if its last product is removed. A day with no sales does not exist and is not listed.
- A product whose retail price is `0` cannot be added, so a stored day always has a total above `0`.
- Simultaneous editing from two devices is not a business case and is not handled.

## 4. Out of scope

- Invoices, invoice numbering, customers, and printing a retail ticket.
- Load Sheet and delivery dates. Retail does not enter the Load Sheet.
- Inventory quantities.
- Sending retail sales to Alegra.
- Counting cash at cierre de caja; it only changes the status.
- A notification or badge for pending reviews.
- Retail reports across several days, and per-product history of past days.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- **Detal** is reachable from the main navigation and is separate from invoices and Cargue.
- The first visit of a day shows an empty list. The previous day's rows are not carried over.
- Picking a product from the inline list does not add it. Typing a quantity and pressing **+ Agregar** adds it. Adding a product already on the list increases its row.
- The table columns are: Código, Marca, Producto, Precio de venta detal unidad, Cantidad, plus a delete action.
- Editing a row quantity saves it and updates both totals, above and below the list.
- Closing the session or opening another device shows today's list unchanged.
- A new retail sale is `UNREVIEWED`. **Cerrar caja** moves the selected days to `REVIEWED` in one action; a second review or going back is rejected.
- Selecting days shows their accumulated total sold and total profit before confirming.
- Today's sale list has no review action.
- After today is reviewed, today cannot be edited and its list is no longer shown.
- A retail sale left unreviewed for several days can still be reviewed.
- Days views: **Sin revisar** by default and **Revisados**, 10 per page, using the same pagination as existing tables.
- Removing the last product of today deletes the day; a day without sales never appears.
- The API rejects any change to a day that is not today or is reviewed. No endpoint changes the date.
- Wholesale sales, invoices, and the Load Sheet behave exactly as before.

## 6. Decisions

| Id | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| OQ-R1 | Integer or decimal quantity? | Integer. |
| OQ-R2 | Frozen or live retail price? | Frozen when the product is added. |
| OQ-R3 | One retail sale per business or per seller? | Per business. |
| OQ-R4 | Removing rows? | Delete button per row. Adding is a two-step pick + quantity + **Agregar**. |
| OQ-R5 | Product without retail cost? | Profit `0`, warning tooltip. |
| OQ-R6 | Keep zero rows at day end? | Moot; detail is discarded. |
| OQ-R7 | Review today? | Yes, from the Sin revisar view only. Once reviewed it is closed for the rest of the day. Reviews are done by selecting days and confirming once. |
| OQ-R8 | Days with total `0`? | Never stored. The day is deleted when its last product is removed. Retail price `0` cannot be added. |
| OQ-R13 | Adding a product already on the list? | Quantity is added to the existing row. |
| OQ-R14 | Row quantity `0`? | Not allowed; minimum `1`. Removal is the delete button, without confirmation. |
| OQ-R9 | Count cash at cierre de caja? | No. Only the status changes. |
| OQ-R10 | Pending-review notice? | No badge. Previous days default to the Sin revisar view, paginated by 10. |
| OQ-R11 | Detail after reviewing today? | Discarded at review. |
| OQ-R12 | Losing per-product detail of past days? | Accepted. |
